# AdultingOS closed testing — tester guide

Thank you for helping test AdultingOS! This is a pre-release version. Please use it for **at least 14 days** and stay opted in the whole time (Google Play requires this before the app can launch).

## Join
1. Accept the invitation e-mail, or open the opt-in link we sent: `[closed testing opt-in URL]`.
2. Tap **Become a tester**, then install **AdultingOS** from Google Play using the same Google account.
3. Keep the app installed and stay opted in for 14 days. Don't leave the test early.

## What to try (about 10 minutes, then use it normally)
- Finish onboarding (all questions are optional).
- Add one real or made-up **bill** and mark it paid. Check that the next due date looks right.
- Track a **document** with an expiry date and check its reminders in Profile → Notifications.
- Open **Guides**, pick one and tap **Add steps to my tasks**.
- Create an account and try **Ask Adu** with a question about adulting.
- Turn on **dark mode** and **bigger text** in your phone settings and look around.
- Try it with **Wi-Fi and data off**.

Please use **made-up details** for anything sensitive. Never enter full ID numbers, passwords or card numbers.

## Report problems
- In the app: **Profile → Feedback and reports**.
- Wrong guide info: open the guide → **Report outdated or wrong information**.
- Bad AI answer: **Report this answer** under it.
- Or e-mail `[support e-mail]` using the template in `feedback-template.md`.

We will share what we fixed in the release notes. Thank you!
