# AdultingOS 1.0.0 (version code 1)

## Play Console "What's new" (≤ 500 chars)

Welcome to AdultingOS! Track tasks, bills, subscriptions and document renewals in one place, with friendly reminders before things are due. Follow step-by-step checklists, ask Adu (AI) for help organizing, and pick your Adulting Buddy. Works offline, with or without an account. No ads.

## Included
- Home dashboard computed from your real data; companion messages; 7-day timeline; monthly progress
- Tasks: create/edit, categories (incl. custom), priority, due date/time, daily–yearly and custom repeats, checklists, tags, notes, search, filters, sorting, bulk complete/archive/delete, archive/restore, reschedule, snooze, skip
- Documents: 15 templates, stages, expiry status, renewal reminders (90/60/30/7/0 days), renewal history, last-4 reference only
- Bills: amounts in centavos, variable amounts, payment history, monthly history, by category; mark paid → exactly one next occurrence
- Subscriptions: monthly/annual equivalents, trials, recorded cancellation
- Calendar: month/week/agenda with category filters
- Guides: general guides; government guides only when verified and published by editors
- Ask Adu (account): grounded answers with citations, uncertainty notices, task proposals with confirmation, reporting, history and deletion
- Reminders: per-category, quiet hours, weekly summary, private lock-screen text, Mark done / Snooze actions
- Settings: profile, appearance (dark mode, reduced motion, hide mascots), notifications, privacy, JSON/CSV export, account deletion, help, feedback, legal, licenses
- Adulting Milestones (optional)

## Not in this release (behind disabled flags)
Savings goals, life milestones, household, routines, premium subscription, encrypted document vault.

## Known limitations
- Filipino translation covers navigation and onboarding only.
- Government guides depend on editorial verification (none verified at the time of writing).
- Reminders may arrive a few minutes late (no exact-alarm permission, by design).
